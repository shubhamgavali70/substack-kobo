How to send links here from your phone

Android: open this page in Chrome, choose “Add to Home screen” (install). The app then appears in the Share sheet; sharing a Substack post opens this page with the link filled in.

iOS: share from the Substack app → “Copy link”, then open this page and paste. Or create a Shortcut that accepts URLs and sends them to POST /api/articles with your API token (see docs/MOBILE.md).
